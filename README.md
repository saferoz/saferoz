# Hi, I'm Raden 👋

**Pilot · AI-native product builder · Visual storyteller**

I'm based in Dammam, Saudi Arabia. I work where aviation, software, and media meet—turning technical operations into digital products and stories people can understand, trust, and use.

My path into technology is unconventional. I bring real operational context, product thinking, and visual direction, then work with frontier AI coding agents to turn ideas into functioning systems. I own the product behavior, decisions, testing, and final quality.

## What I'm building

**[RYDAN](https://rydan.radenhanifa.com)** — an aviation technology initiative focused on calm, trustworthy, and traceable preflight workflows for Saudi general aviation.

**AI-native digital products** — practical tools built from domain insight: aviation workflows, spatial real-estate experiences, interactive data products, and internal systems.

**Visual stories** — aviation and brand films that make complex environments clear without losing their energy, precision, or human side.

## A little context

- GACA Private Pilot Licence and Instrument Rating, with 300 flight hours on the DA40 NG.
- Aviation media production at OxfordSaudia Flight Academy since 2022.
- Selected production work across aviation, automotive, corporate, lifestyle, and cultural brands—including Saudia, OxfordSaudia, and Cadillac.
- Marketing and Creative Director for Indonesia Fashion Night 2025 at the Embassy of Indonesia in Riyadh.
